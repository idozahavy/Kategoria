2. HIGH room: phone reload mid-round lands on Home (or on an old hosted game if that device
   ever hosted) instead of rejoining its seat; typed answers are lost (P4). Rejoin via
   Join works and keeps the seat.

   objet ?", en "a real objet" (P2, P4).

5. MED custom category can duplicate a built-in one ("חיה" twice) (P2).
6. MED "Me" placeholder looks like a default name but is empty; Next blocked (P1).
7. MED endless "Connection lost - reconnecting..." with no way out (P4).
8. LOW speed-bonus -1s unexplained on review; standings "+35 40 75" unlabeled (P1, P2, P4).
9. LOW solo vs robot still shows "Pass the device - no peeking!" (P1).
10. LOW phone header stays "Join a game" all game; fr "1 connectes" plural (P4).
11. LOW blank answer shows "-" with no "Empty · 0" label; results keep the voting heading (P2).
